Questions

Is this a shopping service?

No. We do not buy or ship products for you. You get a list. You buy where you already shop.

Will this save me money?

Sometimes. The point is to stop spending on things you will not repurchase. Prices move; the list is research, not a guarantee.

How do I cancel?

Use the customer portal link in your Lemon Squeezy receipt email. No phone call.
