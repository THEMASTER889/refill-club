# Refill Club — September 2026

Send this (or a PDF of it) to members after they subscribe. Update the prices before you send — they move.

This is research, not a shopping guarantee. Woolworths, Coles, Amazon, and Chemist Warehouse change specials weekly.

## Buy this month (true refills)

1. **Dishwasher tablets, own-brand or a known concentrate pack**  
   People run out on a 4–8 week cycle. Do not buy a novelty scent pod that costs double. Check the per-wash price, not the box price.

2. **Pet dental chews or a diet they already eat (if you have a pet)**  
   Non-negotiable demand. Stay on the same SKU. Switching food to chase a deal often means waste and a sick animal.

3. **Body wash or bar soap you already like**  
   30–90 day empty. Multipacks beat travel minis. Skip “new formula” relaunches unless your current bottle is gone.

## Subscribe if you already use it

- Coffee pods or ground coffee you drink daily — only if you will finish the bag before it goes stale.  
- Toothpaste and floss — boring, light to ship, you will need another tube.  
- Vacuum bags / water filter if you own the machine. Match the model number exactly.

## Skip

- TikTok mops, neck fans, and “as seen on” gadgets. No refill cycle.  
- Giant toilet-paper bales if Coles/Woolies is cheaper after Amazon postage.  
- Variety snack boxes. People get bored and cancel.

## One rule

If you would not buy the same thing again in 60 days, it is not a refill. Do not put it on Subscribe & Save.
