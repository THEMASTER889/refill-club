# Your September restock list

Hey — here's this month's list. Ten minutes to read, worth it if it saves you buying the wrong thing again.

Quick note: this is our own research, not a guarantee — Woolworths, Coles, Amazon, and Chemist Warehouse change their specials every week, so double-check the price before you buy.

## Buy this month (true refills)

**Dishwasher tablets** — own-brand or a known concentrate pack. You'll run out on a 4–8 week cycle either way. Skip the novelty scent pod that costs double — check the per-wash price, not the box price.

**Pet food or dental chews (if you have a pet)** — stick to the same one your pet already eats. Switching to chase a deal usually means wasted food or an upset stomach.

**Body wash or bar soap you already like** — these run out every 30–90 days regardless. Multipacks beat travel minis. Skip "new formula" relaunches until your current bottle is actually empty.

## Worth putting on subscribe & save

- **Coffee** (pods or ground) — only if you'll finish the bag before it goes stale.
- **Toothpaste and floss** — boring, light to ship, you'll need more anyway.
- **Vacuum bags / water filters** — if you own the machine. Match the model number exactly.

## Skip this month

- Viral gadgets (mops, neck fans, "as seen on TV" stuff) — no real refill cycle, they just sit in a drawer.
- Giant toilet-paper bales — often cheaper at Coles/Woolies once you account for postage.
- Variety snack boxes — people get bored of them and cancel.

## The one rule

If you wouldn't buy the exact same thing again within 60 days, it's not a refill — don't put it on repeat order.

See you next month.
